# Pricing & Business Model

GymKit is designed to support two distinct commercial models:

1. **White-label Template Sale**: Sold as a one-time hand-off to a gym owner. They receive the source code, their specific PocketBase data, and instructions to host it themselves.
2. **Managed Service (SaaS)**: Hosted centrally by us. We spin up a new PocketBase container for each client and charge a monthly subscription. The Flutter web app dynamically routes to their container based on their custom domain.
