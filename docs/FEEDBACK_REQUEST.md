# 📣 GymKit - Ideas & Feedback Request

Hello! 👋 

We are currently building **GymKit**, a premium, white-label Gym Management SaaS platform. It's designed to be a highly customizable mobile and web app that gym owners can deploy as their own branded application (config-over-code).

We have just completed our **Phase 1 Prototype** (the core infrastructure, theme engine, and secure backend), and before we finalize the specific workflows for our V1 release, **we need your feedback and ideas!**

---

## 📖 Context: What are we building?
To understand the scope of what we are creating, please take a quick look at these two documents:

1. **[REQUIREMENTS.md](requirements/REQUIREMENTS.md)**: This explains our core philosophy, what we are trying to achieve, and the strict boundaries of our V1 (MVP) vs V2 scopes.
2. **[feature_matrix.md](requirements/feature_matrix.md)**: This is a matrix showing exactly what each role (`Owner`, `Manager`, `Receptionist`, `Trainer`, `Member`) can currently do in the app.

---

## 🧠 What we need from you

We want to ensure that GymKit solves real-world problems for gym staff and members without becoming bloated. Please review the documents above and consider the following questions:

### 1. The Member Experience
- Looking at the member features (QR check-in, viewing active plans), is there a critical "Day 1" feature we are missing that members absolutely expect from a modern gym app?
- How should we handle members who forget their phones for QR check-in? 

### 2. The Staff / Desk Experience
- For a `Receptionist` or `Manager` running the front desk, what is the biggest friction point in daily operations? Does our proposed QR scanning workflow address it?
- Are the current offline/cash payment tracking features sufficient, or are there edge cases in gym payments we haven't considered?

### 3. Analytics & Growth (Owner Experience)
- As an `Owner`, what are the top 3 metrics you need to see the second you open the Dashboard?
- What kind of push notifications actually drive member retention without feeling spammy?

### 4. Wildcard Ideas
- Forget the current scope for a second—if you could magically add one killer feature to a gym app that you've never seen done well, what would it be?
